Ventilation Solutions: Centrifugal & Axial Fans

Centrifugal and axial fans ensure efficient air circulation and ventilation with high performance. These fans are engineered for maximum airflow with minimal energy consumption, making them ideal for cleanroom environments and industrial facilities.

Variable Air Volume (VAV) Units

VAV systems automatically adjust airflow based on demand, improving energy efficiency and maintaining consistent indoor air quality while reducing operational costs.

Hydronic Units with Valve Stations

Hydronic units are designed for precise temperature control, using water-based cooling and heating for efficient thermal management. Integrated valve stations further enhance flow regulation and system performance.

Basement Ventilation Solutions

Basement ventilation solutions are built to ensure proper airflow and exhaust management, preventing stale air buildup, humidity issues and any sort of air quality degradation in underground spaces.

Air Distribution Products

Air distribution products, including a comprehensive range like ducting, diffusers and air grilles to ensure uniform airflow and optimal air circulation in cleanroom and industrial environments.

Constant Volume Regulators

Constant Volume Regulators (CVRs) help maintain steady airflow, ensuring consistent air pressure and cleanroom integrity.

Flexible Duct Connectors

Flexible duct connectors minimize vibration and noise in HVAC systems, enhancing overall efficiency and durability.
