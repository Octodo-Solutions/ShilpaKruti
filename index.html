Services

At Shilpakruti, we understand the critical importance of maintaining a contamination-free cleanroom environment.

Our extensive testing, validation and certification services ensure compliance with the regulatory guidelines.

Using advanced technology and expert analysis, we assess air quality, filtration efficiency, temperature, humidity
and overall cleanroom performance to maintain industry standards.

Air Velocity & ACPH

Maintaining the proper air exchange rate is crucial to prevent contamination and ensure uniform airflow distribution.

HEPA/ULPA Filter Integrity Testing

High-efficiency particulate air (HEPA) and ultra-low penetration air (ULPA) filters are the backbone of cleanroom contamination control.

Temperature Mapping

Cleanrooms in pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and healthcare industries require precise temperature control to protect sensitive processes.
